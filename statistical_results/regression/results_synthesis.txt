### GLMM Interaction note

The interaction term C(condition)[T.AB]:C(target_type)[T.EM] captures
whether the EM-vs-BB accuracy difference is the same in both conditions.
  OR > 1: EM advantage is LARGER in AB than NB
  OR < 1: EM advantage is SMALLER in AB (or BB advantage is larger in AB)
  Simple effects:
    Effect of condition for BB trials = main effect of condition only
    Effect of condition for EM trials = main effect + interaction coefficient
