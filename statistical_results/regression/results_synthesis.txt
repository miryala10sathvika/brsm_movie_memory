### 1) Model 3 Result (Participant-Level baseline: aggregated binomial GLM)
This model predicts each participant’s overall accuracy (successes vs failures), using **Condition + Age + Gender**.

- **Condition (NB vs AB):** NB significantly improves odds of overall accuracy vs AB  
  - `condition_NB` OR = 1.299, p = 0.00018  
  - Interpretation: NB has ~29.9% higher odds of correct responses than AB (equivalently, AB is worse).
- **Gender (Male vs Female):** Male shows lower overall accuracy odds  
  - OR = 0.786, p = 0.00543 (~21.4% lower odds).
- **Age:** borderline / not clearly significant  
  - OR = 0.966 per year, p = 0.061.

Saved in: model3_participant_glm_odds_ratios.csv

### 2) Model 1 Result (Trial-Level clustered logistic regression: GEE; “new” expanded models)
We fit a systematic family of GEE logistic models (clustered by participant), then selected the **best stable** model by QIC. The winner is:

- **Best stable model:** `M1_stim_main` (lowest QIC among stable fits)  
  - QIC table: model1_model_comparison_qic.csv

Key scientific effects in `M1_stim_main` (odds of a correct trial):

- **Condition (AB vs NB):** AB reduces accuracy odds  
  - OR = 0.775, p = 0.00845 (≈22.5% lower odds), 95% CI [0.641, 0.937]
- **Target Type (EM vs BB):** EM targets are remembered better than BB  
  - OR = 1.233, p = 0.00154 (≈23.3% higher odds), 95% CI [1.083, 1.404]
- **Repeat (is_repeat = 1 vs 0):** repeats are much easier  
  - OR = 1.514, p = 0.000121 (≈51.4% higher odds), 95% CI [1.226, 1.871]
- **Stimulus duration (movie_duration):** longer duration predicts higher accuracy odds  
  - OR = 1.022 per 1-unit duration, p ≈ 7e-11, 95% CI [1.016, 1.029]
- **Demographics:** Male is slightly lower (p = 0.049), age is not clearly significant (p = 0.098). Vision difficulty shows a large OR but should be interpreted cautiously (likely small subgroup).

Saved OR table: M1_stim_main_odds_ratios.csv
